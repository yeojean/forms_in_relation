FORMS IN RELATION

Plasticity As a Record of Contact

This website translates a physical publication into an interactive digital space. It looks at urban objects as forms that receive pressure, break through weathering and fracture, and are given form again through repair, recycling, and adaptation.

The interface follows the same logic. Images compress, rupture, split and reassemble. The user does not only read about change; interaction becomes one of the forces that changes the form.
